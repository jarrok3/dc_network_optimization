Cheap criterion G versus full evaluation E precomputed

G = DC fixed cost + λ × capacitated assignment cost on road distance. E = the full evaluation (routing on sampled demand days). Prediction quality (how well G ranks the candidates) and decision quality (what the G-best candidate loses under E) are different numbers.
